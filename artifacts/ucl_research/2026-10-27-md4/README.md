# UCL Matchday 4 research freeze

As of 2026-10-27, the shared Clubalpha intelligence stack has been run across
all 36 Champions League league-phase clubs for Matchday 4.

- The same projected-XI Player Alpha formulas, five-match selection window,
  three-lineup formation vote, 990-minute allocation, and 60/30/10 fixture
  foundation used in the Premier League are preserved.
- Matchdays 1-3 contribute 54 completed observational matches: 221 goals,
  189.58 xG and 40 Over 2.5 results.
- Projected-XI Alpha covers 34/36 clubs. Sabah FK and Shakhtar Donetsk remain
  explicitly unrated instead of receiving an invented low grade.
- Matchday 4 contains 18 fixtures and 900,000 total simulations.
- Barcelona over Aston Villa and Borussia Dortmund over Real Betis clear the
  raw shared side thresholds. Fifteen raw Over 2.5 candidates also clear the
  shared total threshold.
- None of these candidates is an official call. The UCL bucket does not yet
  have its own venue, draw, and tempo calibration, so every candidate remains
  research-only and capital deployment remains disabled.

The first honest UCL forecast ledger begins only after the Matchday 4 slate is
refreshed and frozen before kickoff. Matchdays 1-3 must never be retroactively
scored as predictions.
